About Kickresume

Kickresume is a real company you can trust.

For over a decade, Kickresume has been the resume builder job seekers trust to tell their story. Named the #1 resume builder overall by Forbes, we’ve helped more than 9 million people worldwide land roles at the world’s most sought-after companies: from Google to Apple and SpaceX.

Today's Kickresume is a full AI-powered career toolkit, helping candidates write standout resumes and cover letters, pass ATS checks, prepare for interviews, and even plan their next career move.

Our work has been recognized and cited by media such as The Wall Street Journal, CNBC, Fast Company, Business Insider, and the New York Post. We're also affiliated with the PARWCC (Professional Association of Resume Writers & Career Coaches), with who we even did a joint research study.

We’re a small, passionate team based in Bratislava, Slovakia. And every morning, we show up to do one thing: help people land the right jobs.

Straight answers to what people actually ask about Kickresume.

Is Kickresume legit?

Yes. Kickresume is run by Kickresume s.r.o., a company registered in Slovakia (ID 48 073 962) and operating since 2013.

Who owns Kickresume?

Kickresume is owned by its founders, Peter Ďuriš and Tomáš Ondrejka. It's bootstrapped, with no parent company and no sister brands.

Where is Kickresume based?

Kickresume is headquartered in Bratislava, Slovakia, and operates under EU data protection law (GDPR).

Does Kickresume share my resume with recruiters?

Only if you opt in. Kickresume keeps your documents private by default and shares them with hiring partners only with your consent.

Is Kickresume really free?

Kickresume has a free plan with no time limit. Premium features need a paid subscription, which you can cancel in your account settings.

How do I delete my Kickresume data?

Close your account and Kickresume deletes your data after 30 days. For anything else, email gdpr@kickresume.com.
