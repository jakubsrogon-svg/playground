01 / Private by default

Your job search stays your business.

Looking for a new job while you still have one? We get it. Your resumes and other career documents aren’t publicly accessible, listed in a recruiter database, or passed on to employers.

  • No recruiter database. We don’t collect resumes for employers to browse, and we don’t share yours with hiring companies.

  • Access-controlled by default. Your documents are tied to your account and can’t be opened without signing in.

  • Link sharing you control. Your personal website stays private until you enable link sharing. You can disable it anytime.

02 / Protected by EU law

The EU takes privacy seriously. We do too.

As a company based in Europe, we’re bound by the GDPR, one of the strictest privacy laws in the world. That protection covers every Kickresume user, wherever they live.

  • Full control over your data. Want a copy? A correction? Everything deleted? It’s your right, and we’ll make it happen.

  • Real person, real answers. Our data protection officer handles every privacy question at gdpr@kickresume.com.

  • EU-level protection, worldwide. So even if you’re in the US, how we collect, use and protect your data meets EU standards.

03 / Security on every level

Our security gets a yearly check-up.

Independent security experts test our systems for vulnerabilities yearly. We host your data on certified servers and limit team access to those whose work requires it.

  • Annual pen tests and audits. Independent experts probe our systems to uncover weak spots before they can be exploited.

  • Certified hosting. Our servers run on AWS and Akamai infrastructure, certified to standards like ISO 27001 and SOC 2.

  • Restricted team access. Not everyone at Kickresume can see your data. Only the people who need it for their work.

[VISUAL: Security on every level]

04 / Never used to train AI

Your resume isn’t training data. Ever.

Our AI tools help you write a better resume, but your documents never become training material. We don’t train AI on them, and neither do our providers, OpenAI and Anthropic.

  • OpenAI. We explicitly opted out, so OpenAI doesn’t use your data to train its models.

  • Anthropic. The policy is not to train on business data, so your documents stay out of its models.

  • Kickresume. The data we build and test with is synthetic and isn’t linked to any real person.

[VISUAL: Never used to train AI]

05 / Data never sold

We sell subscriptions, not your data.

Some companies make money from your data. We don’t. We never sell it to advertisers or data brokers, and never share it with recruiters or companies for their own use.

  • Never shared with employers. Not now, not ever. Your resume has never been passed to recruiters or hiring companies.

  • Data processing. Providers that help run Kickresume handle your data strictly on our instructions. They’re named in our privacy policy.

  • Legally required only. Beyond our service providers, we disclose data only when legally required, for example by a court order.

[VISUAL: Data never sold]

06 / Deleted within 30 days

When you leave, your data leaves too.

Close your account anytime and your account data is deleted after 30 days. Inactive accounts are cleaned up after 5 years, and your card details are never stored at all.

  • 30 days. You can delete your account whenever you like. After 30 days, everything in it is permanently erased.

  • 5 years. Many services keep inactive accounts forever. We don’t. Stop logging in, we’ll email you, then delete your account.

  • Card details never stored. Payments are handled entirely by our payment provider.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kickresume safe to use?

Yes. Kickresume is an EU-based company, so it’s bound by the GDPR, one of the strictest privacy laws in the world. Our systems get independent penetration tests every year, your data is hosted on ISO 27001 and SOC 2 certified infrastructure, and access to your data is limited to team members who need it for their work.

Does Kickresume sell my data or share my resume with employers?

No. Kickresume doesn’t sell your personal data to advertisers, data brokers or anyone else. We’ve never shared resumes with employers or recruiters. Your documents stay private in your account until you choose to share them yourself.

Will my current employer find out I’m using Kickresume?

Not from us. Your resumes and cover letters aren’t public, aren’t listed in any database for recruiters to search, and aren’t passed on to employers. Your personal website only goes live if you turn on link sharing, and you can switch it off anytime.

Does Kickresume use my resume to train AI?

No. Kickresume doesn’t train AI on your documents. Our AI providers don’t either: we’ve opted out of training with OpenAI, and Anthropic doesn’t train on business customers’ data by default.

How do I delete my Kickresume account and data?

You can delete your account anytime in your settings, on the web or in the mobile app. Once you do, everything in your account is permanently deleted after a 30-day grace period. Accounts that stay inactive for 5 years are deleted automatically, after an email warning.
